About Vobux International

Building global fashion partnerships through quality, trust, and innovation.

Our Story

Years of Excellence

VOBUX International is a global sourcing and export company specializing in garments, footwear, bags, and fashion accessories. As a proud Group Company of M.BUX International (Hong Kong) and a Branch Company of Namo International Ltd, we connect world-class Chinese manufacturers with international buyers across: • GCC Countries • Europe • India With years of experience in manufacturing, sourcing, quality control, and international logistics, we help brands and wholesalers build successful businesses through reliable supply chains.

Innovation drives everything we do. Our R&D team continuously develops new fashion collections by analyzing: • International Fashion Trends • Material Innovation • Consumer Preferences • Sustainability • Cost Optimization We work closely with designers and manufacturers to transform ideas into market-ready products. Our Services include: • Product Design • Sample Development • Material Selection • Pattern Making • Packaging Design • Private Label Development

Quality is our commitment. Every shipment passes through: • Raw Material Inspection • Inline Inspection • Final Inspection • Packing Inspection • Container Loading Supervision Our goal is zero-defect delivery.

VOBUX International believes responsible manufacturing is essential for long-term success. We are committed to: • Sustainable Materials • Eco-Friendly Production • Ethical Manufacturing • Reduced Carbon Footprint • Responsible Packaging • Long-term Supplier Partnerships Together with our manufacturing partners, we strive to build a greener future. We proudly export to: • United Arab Emirates • Saudi Arabia • Qatar • Kuwait • Bahrain • Oman • India
