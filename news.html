News Namo International Limited - Strengthens Global Garment & Footwear Sourcing Across China, India, Europe and the GCC Namo International Ltd is expanding its role as an international sourcing and trading partner, connecting trusted manufacturers with buyers across the GCC, Europe and India. As global apparel and footwear supply chains continue to evolve, international buyers are increasingly looking for reliable manufacturing partners, competitive pricing, consistent quality and efficient cross-border sourcing. Namo International Ltd aims to support buyers by creating a trusted bridge between manufacturers and international markets. As a Group Company of M.BUX International (Hong Kong) and a Branch Company of Namo International Ltd, our business network is focused on developing strong international trade relationships and creating new opportunities across the garment and footwear sectors. Connecting Manufacturers With Global Buyers Namo International works to connect buyers with suitable manufacturers and suppliers for a wide range of products, including: Ready-made garments Fashion apparel Workwear and uniforms Sportswear Casualwear Footwear Shoes and related products Private-label and OEM sourcing Our objective is to make international sourcing more efficient by helping buyers identify suitable manufacturing partners and develop reliable long-term supply relationships. Growing Opportunities Across International Markets The global textile and apparel industry continues to present opportunities for manufacturers and international buyers. India, for example, recorded strong overall textile and apparel export activity in FY2025–26, with important markets including the UAE and several European countries showing significant trade volumes. At the same time, European buyers are increasingly exploring diversified sourcing strategies. Recent industry developments show growing interest in expanding apparel exports toward European markets, creating opportunities for international sourcing companies with established supplier networks. These developments highlight the importance of having reliable partners who understand both manufacturing capabilities and international buyer requirements. Our Focus: Quality, Reliability and International Trade Namo International Ltd is committed to building business relationships based on: Quality — Working with manufacturers capable of meeting international buyer requirements. Competitive Sourcing — Helping buyers identify suitable products and manufacturing options at competitive market prices. Reliability — Supporting long-term relationships between suppliers and international buyers. Global Connectivity — Creating opportunities between manufacturers in Asia and buyers across the GCC, Europe and India. Professional Service — Supporting communication and coordination throughout the sourcing and export process. Building Long-Term Global Partnerships Our vision is not simply to facilitate individual transactions. We aim to develop long-term international business partnerships between manufacturers, suppliers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers and buyers. With our international business network and market-oriented approach, Namo International Ltd is positioned to support companies seeking dependable garment and footwear sourcing opportunities across key global markets. Partner With Namo International Whether you are an international buyer looking for reliable garment and footwear manufacturers or a manufacturer seeking new international markets, Namo International Ltd welcomes opportunities to build mutually beneficial business relationships.
